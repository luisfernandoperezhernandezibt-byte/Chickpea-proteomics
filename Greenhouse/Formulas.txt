DLI = Rs × f × C

DLI = daily light integral (mol·m⁻²·d⁻¹)
Rs  = broadband solar radiation (MJ·m⁻²·d⁻¹)
f   = PAR energy fraction of shortwave radiation (0.45)
C   = sunlight photon conversion factor (4.57 mol·MJ⁻¹)

March : DLI = 23.70 × 0.45 × 4.57 = 48.7
April : DLI = 25.06 × 0.45 × 4.57 = 51.5
May   : DLI = 24.68 × 0.45 × 4.57 = 50.8
Mean (March–May) : DLI = (48.7 + 51.5 + 50.8) / 3 = 50.3


Rs[W·m⁻²] = Rs[MJ·m⁻²·d⁻¹] × 10⁶ / 86400

Rs    = solar radiation
10⁶   = MJ to J conversion (J·MJ⁻¹)
86400 = seconds per day (s·d⁻¹)

March : Rs = 23.70 × 10⁶ / 86400 = 274.3
April : Rs = 25.06 × 10⁶ / 86400 = 290.0
May   : Rs = 24.68 × 10⁶ / 86400 = 285.6


DLI = PFD × Photoperiod × 3600 × 10⁻⁶

DLI        = daily light integral (mol·m⁻²·d⁻¹)
PFD        = photon flux density (µmol·m⁻²·s⁻¹)
Photoperiod = daily light duration (h)
3600       = seconds per hour (s·h⁻¹)
10⁻⁶       = µmol to mol conversion (mol·µmol⁻¹)

LED : DLI = 274.3 × 16 × 3600 × 10⁻⁶ = 15.8
